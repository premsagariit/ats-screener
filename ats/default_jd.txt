 AI Engineer (Graduate Hire)

About Niva Bupa
At Niva Bupa, our purpose is to give every Indian the confidence to access the best healthcare.
We are a certified Great Place to Work, covering more than 22 million lives across a network of
10,400+ hospitals. As part of the global Bupa healthcare group, we empower our customers to
take control of their health and live life to the fullest.

About the AI Lab
The AI Lab at Niva Bupa builds enterprise-grade Generative AI and Agentic Systems that power
the core of our health insurance operations. Our mission is to transform claims automation,
underwriting intelligence, care journeys, sales productivity, and next-generation customer
experiences using deep tech. As a member joining as an AI Engineer, you will work
at the intersection of advanced AI (LLMs, agentic systems), large-scale enterprise systems, and
direct business impact.

The Opportunity
  • Massive impact: The work you do will directly influence the health and well-being of
    millions of Indians. This is AI with a purpose.
  • True full-stack ownership: You won't be a cog in a wheel. You will see your projects all
    the way from research to production.
  • The best of both worlds: The innovative, fast-paced culture of a tech start-up, backed by
    the stability and resources of a market leader.

What You'll Work On
1. Generative AI Core Engineering & Agent Orchestration
  • Help architect and deploy multi-agent workflows using modern orchestration frameworks
    (LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Bedrock, Vertex AI) to solve complex, multi-step business
    problems.
  • Design and build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, fine-tuned LLMs, high-
    performance embeddings pipelines, and document intelligence solutions.
  • Contribute to productionizing LLM-based agents for claims validation, pre-authorization,
    sales advisory, care management, and internal AI copilots.
  • Support work on advanced models, including transformers, diffusion models, and multi-
    modal (vision, speech) models for specialized health-tech challenges.


2. Full-Stack Product Engineering
  • Work across the entire UI → API → Model workflow, ensuring seamless, high-
    performance integration from front-end to inference.
  • Build user interfaces using modern frameworks (Next.js, React, Streamlit) for both
    customer-facing and internal operational tools.
  • Develop scalable backend microservices (Python/FastAPI, Node.js/Express) optimized for
    LLM inference and large-scale data serving.


3. LLMOps & Deployment
  • Ship models to production using modern MLOps tooling (MLflow, CI/CD, Docker,
    Kubernetes).
  • Help optimize latency, throughput, inference cost, and security for deployed GenAI
    services.
  • Build monitoring and observability for GenAI: model drift, hallucination and toxicity rates,
    guardrail/compliance violations, and agent decision tracing.


What You Bring to the Table
Must-Haves
  • Strong fundamentals in algorithms, data structures, distributed systems, and applied
    Machine Learning / Deep Learning.
  • Hands-on exposure to LLMs and at least one agent/orchestration framework
    (LangChain/LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Bedrock, or Vertex) through projects, internships, or
    research.
  • Working proficiency in Python; familiarity with a modern web stack (React/Next.js front-
    end, FastAPI or Node.js back-end).
  • A builder's mindset — comfortable in ambiguous, “0 to 1” environments where you help
     define both the product and the architecture.
Preferred
  • Experience with multi-modal models (vision, speech) relevant to healthcare/insurance
    documentation.
  • Exposure to fine-tuning LLMs or training custom small models for domain-specific tasks.
  • Interest in or exposure to healthcare, insurance, or financial services domains.
  • Familiarity with optimizing LLM inference (quantization, compilation, specialized
    hardware).
